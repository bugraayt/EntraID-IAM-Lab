## What I did 
Created 5 user accounts in Microsoft Entra ID representing a small simulated organization, each assigned a realistic job title and department to reflect distinct access needs.

## Why it matters 
Establishing user identities with department/role metadata is the foundation of least-privilege access — you can't assign appropriate permissions without first knowing who someone is and what they do.
